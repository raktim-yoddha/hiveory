# ADR 0042 — Feedback round 11: browser panes, tabbed panes, renaming, avatars, annotations

Builds on ADR 0040 and 0041. Decided by the product owner, 2026-10-11.

## Context

The product owner asked for the browser to live among the panes, Orca-style tabbed pane areas chosen
at onboarding, renaming panes, image avatars and any color for a Workspace, batches of browser
annotations sent to an agent, and a lighter side panel. Asked before building: in the tabbed style a
new agent opens as a tab in the current area; a double left click on a pane or tab title maximizes
it (a double right click renames it); Routines and Workspace settings become side-panel tabs of their
own, not open by default.

## Decision

### Side panel
- A worktree's side panel starts with Dashboard, Sessions and Explorer.
- Routines and Workspace settings are side-panel tabs of their own (from "+", or a Workspace's
  right-click › Workspace settings); the Dashboard keeps Tasks, Worktrees and Issues.
- The Dashboard's section tabs have icons and show only them while the panel is narrow (Tabs
  `iconsWhenNarrow`, a container query); the side panel's own tabs do the same.

### Browser panes
- A browser page is a pane of its worktree, beside agents, terminals and files: same header, drag,
  split, swap and maximize. A pane's "+" offers Browser (placed right or below that pane); pages an
  agent opens join its worktree's layout as panes; Queen Bee opens them as panes too.
- The side panel no longer holds browsers. Main counts a worktree's pages among its panes
  (`AgentService.setExtraPanes`, `BrowserService.pageIdsIn`) and reconciles the layout when one opens
  or closes (`onScopeChange`); `browser.open` takes `beside` to place it.
- The page is a native view: it hides while another pane is maximized and steps aside (leaving a
  picture) while a pane is dragged.

### Browser notes in batches
- Annotate keeps going: click an element, write the note, choose **Change | Question**, Add (Enter);
  the next click annotates the next element, until Done or Escape. Each note's number appears on its
  element in the page (badges the page script draws, again after a reload).
- The page's notes form one batch under the toolbar: numbered rows (element, note, change or
  question, remove), **Send** (to a running agent of this worktree, or a new agent of any installed
  CLI, which gets them once it is ready), **Copy** and Clear. Sent notes clear. `annotationsText`
  writes the message; agents still read notes with `browser_annotations`, now with each one's intent.

### Tabbed panes (an option, chosen at the first run)
- `paneStyle`: **Tiles** (one pane per area, as before) or **Tabs** (like Orca). The first run shows
  a welcome asking which (`welcomed` records the answer; existing installs see it once too); Settings
  › Appearance › Pane style changes it any time.
- In Tabs, a layout leaf may hold several panes: `{ type: 'pane', paneId: <shown>, tabs: [...] }`.
  Every tab keeps its own slot at the area's rect, mounted (terminals keep running) but out of sight
  unless chosen, so switching tabs never remounts anything. New operations: `stack` (into another
  area as a tab) and `activate` (choose a tab); `remove`, `move`, `swap` and splitting beside a tab
  understand areas. Saved layouts accept `tabs` (it must include the shown pane).
- The area's header becomes a tab strip with the gutters and look of the tiles: each tab has its
  icon or CLI logo, name, status dot and ×; the area's "+" opens Tab (default) · Right · Bottom;
  maximize. Click a tab to show it, drag it to an edge to split it out or into the middle of another
  area to add it there (drop preview "Add as a tab"), middle-click to close it, double-click to
  maximize, right-click for Rename · Split right · Split down · Close, right-click twice to rename.
  Each pane registers what closing it means (`pane-commands`), so a file still asks about unsaved
  changes.
- Back to Tiles, main spreads every area's tabs into side-by-side panes (`flattenTabs`), so none stays
  hidden. Arranging (Equal, Focus, Columns) lays every pane out on its own, in either style.

### Renaming a pane
Right-click an agent's or terminal's pane name twice (or right-click › Rename) to rename it in
place: Enter saves, Escape cancels. The name is the agent's pet name, so it changes everywhere at
once: the sidebar, the board, other agents' tools, Queen Bee and the phone. Names stay unique across
all agents, removed ones included (`agents.rename`). Pet names are still generated by the one
pet-name service; a rename is the user's own choice (AGENTS.md rule 17).

### Workspace avatar and any color
Workspace settings › Icon and color has **Icon | Avatar**. Icon: the icons, the theme colors, and
any color from the system color picker (`customColor`, #rrggbb, over the theme color). Avatar: an
image of the user's own (PNG or JPEG, via `projects.pickAvatar`), cropped to its centre square and
kept as a 96 px PNG data URL in the Workspace's settings, so the sidebar, the Dashboard and the phone
show it without reading files. Choosing an icon goes back from the avatar.

## Consequences

A worktree opened before this keeps the side-panel tabs it had; the defaults apply to new ones.
Browser pages are not restored after a restart (as before), so their panes leave the layout then.
In Tabs, files opened from the Explorer and agents opened by Queen Bee or presets still open in their
own area; only a pane's "+" opens a tab.

## Amendment (2026-10-11): fixed side-panel tabs, Orca-style tab areas, floating notes

Decided by the product owner after trying round 11.

- **Side panel:** every worktree's side panel always has Dashboard, Sessions, Explorer, Routines and
  Settings. There is no "+" and no ×: tabs only drag to reorder, or between the top and bottom area
  for a dual view and back. A worktree opened before gets the tabs it lacks.
